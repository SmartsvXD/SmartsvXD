Computer Engineering undergraduate at the **University of Catania (UNICT)**, affiliated with the **Department of Electrical, Electronics and Computer Engineering (DIEEI)**.

My research and engineering interests focus on **machine learning**, **evolutionary optimization**, and **quantitative modeling**, combined with systems-level programming and embedded hardware.

---

### 🔬 Research & Academic Interests

- **Machine Learning & Deep Learning**: Statistical learning, neural network architectures (PyTorch, TensorFlow, Keras), and experiment tracking (Weights & Biases).
- **Evolutionary Computation & Metaheuristics**: Multi-objective genetic algorithms (NSGA-II, pymoo), Pareto-optimal frontier generation, and constrained numerical optimization.
- **Quantitative Finance & Risk Modeling**: Non-parametric risk estimation (Conditional Value-at-Risk / CVaR, Moving Block Bootstrap), portfolio optimization, and Meucci's Effective Number of Bets (ENB) factor diversification.
- **Embedded Systems & Hardware-Software Co-Design**: Microcontroller firmware (ESP32, Arduino), hardware peripherals, real-time sensing, and edge computing.
